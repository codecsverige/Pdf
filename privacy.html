PDF Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

PDF Pro is designed as a local-first PDF utility. The current production build does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud-processing SDKs.

Files and document content

PDF files, images, signatures, passwords, and document content selected or created in PDF Pro are processed on the device. PDF Pro does not upload those files or their contents to a server operated by the developer.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose the Scan to PDF feature. Images are accessed only when you choose them through the Android system picker for PDF creation. The app does not scan your photo library in the background.

Storage

Generated files are stored in the app cache until you save, download, share, or remove them. If you enable automatic download or select a folder, PDF Pro stores the generated result in that destination at your request.

Data collection and sharing

The current production build does not collect or sell personal data, does not use behavioral advertising, and does not share document content with the developer or advertisers.

Third-party components

PDF Pro uses open-source software libraries for local PDF and Android functionality. These components run as part of the app and are not used by PDF Pro to transmit your documents to a remote service.

Children

PDF Pro is a general utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future release introduces online services, analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the public project page: github.com/codecsverige/Pdf.
